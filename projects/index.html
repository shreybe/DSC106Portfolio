Storms, States, and Severity: U.S. Major Power Outages

DSC 80 · UC San Diego

A data analysis project on major power outages in the continental U.S. (2000–2016), using the Purdue outage dataset. I explore how climate region and outage cause relate to duration, assess missingness in demand loss, run hypothesis tests and permutation analyses, and build baseline and random-forest models to predict outage duration—with a fairness check across climate regions.

miR-214 targeting of TFDP1 and AIFM2 (3′ UTR)

Briganti Lab · Mentor: Pamela Moleri

Research question: Whether miR-214 regulates apoptosis-related genes by binding microRNA response elements in the 3′ UTRs of TFDP1 and AIFM2, leading to translational repression and altered protein expression.

Background

  • TFDP1: In post-mitotic cells, often part of the DREAM complex with E2F4/5, repressing cell-cycle genes; observed downregulation under miR-214–related conditions.
  • AIFM2: Tied to apoptosis and mitochondrial function; expression shifts with miR-214 manipulation and in PLN-R14Del models.

Approach

  • Isolate TFDP1 and AIFM2 3′ UTRs; PCR and agarose-gel verification.
  • Clone into reporter plasmids (restriction/ligation with AsiSI, SpeI / Spel-HF), transform E. coli, prep plasmid, sequence inserts.
  • Transfect HEK cells with luciferase reporters; if miR-214 binds the MRE, luciferase signal drops.
  • Western blot for TFDP1 and AIFM2 protein levels, independent of the reporter assay.

Goals

Validate whether miR-214 directly targets these 3′ UTRs, connect reporter and protein readouts, and clarify how miR-214 may shift apoptosis-related pathways.
