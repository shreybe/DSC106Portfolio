Mentor: Pamela Moleri · 2024 — present

Project: Investigating miR-214–mediated regulation of TFDP1 and AIFM2 via 3′ UTR targeting.

  • Hypothesis: miR-214 binds MREs in target 3′ UTRs, repressing translation and reducing protein output for apoptosis-related genes.
  • Methods: genomic DNA prep, 3′ UTR amplification and cloning, restriction digestion and ligation into luciferase reporter vectors, bacterial transformation and plasmid prep, HEK transfection, luciferase assay, western blot for protein-level validation.
  • Aims: validate direct targeting, confirm MRE function, and relate findings to miR-214– driven changes in stress and apoptosis pathways.
